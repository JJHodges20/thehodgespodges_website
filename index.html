Why a hodgepodge

Most hobbies that involve building and upgrading have the same problem: thousands of parts, a handful of forums, and no easy way to know whether the part in your cart actually fits the thing on your bench.

TheHodgesPodges is a mixed bag on purpose. One workshop, many hobbies, and the same promise in every planner:

Start with what you own
Plans begin from your exact model, not a generic category.
Show the proof
A fit is marked as a fit only when a manufacturer document says so.
Say when we don't know
Anything unproven is labelled that way, so you can decide for yourself.
