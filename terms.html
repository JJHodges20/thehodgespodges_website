Fitment information is a guide

Our apps mark a part as a fit only when a manufacturer document supports it, and they label everything else as not verified. Even so:

  • manufacturers change parts, revisions and packaging, and documents can be wrong or out of date;
  • we can make mistakes when reading or entering information;
  • a "documented fit" covers what the document covers, such as a mounting pattern or dimensions, and not every way two parts interact.

Check fitment against the manufacturer's current instructions before you buy, cut, drill or solder anything. "Not verified" means we found no documented answer, not that a part won't fit.
