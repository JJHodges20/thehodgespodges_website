The short version

  • Browsing TheHodgesPodges needs no account and we don't track you. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies.
  • If you create an account in one of our hobby apps, we keep your email address, a scrambled form of your password, and the builds and wishlists you save, so the app can work for you.
  • We don't sell your personal information or share it for advertising.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data or ask us to delete it at any time.

Who runs this site

TheHodgesPodges and its hobby apps are run by [owner or business name], based in [state], United States. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean that operator, and "you" means anyone who visits the site or uses an app.

This website

The TheHodgesPodges website is a directory of hobbies and planner apps. It has no sign-up, no forms and no comments, so you can't give us personal information through it.

What still gets logged

Like any website, the server that delivers our pages may record basic request information: your IP address, the page asked for, the date and time, and your browser type. Our hosting provider, [hosting provider], keeps these logs for security and to keep the site running, for up to [number] days. We don't use them to build profiles of visitors.

Fonts

The site's typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser asks Google's servers for the font files, which tells Google your IP address and browser type. Google describes how it handles this in its own privacy policy.

Our hobby apps

Each hobby on TheHodgesPodges gets its own planner app. The first, the RC Crawler Build Lab, is being built now. When an app launches, this section describes what it collects. Every app follows the same rules.

What we collect when you use an app

Account details
Your email address and a password. We store the password only as a one-way hash, so nobody, including us, can read it. Accounts are by invitation for now, and we keep a scrambled record of the invitation code you used.
What you plan
The vehicles, instruments or other projects you add, the parts you choose, your saved builds, snapshots and wishlists, and any notes you type into them. Your builds are private to your account unless an app offers sharing and you choose to share.
Sign-in information
When you sign in, we set a cookie that keeps you signed in, and we record when that sign-in expires or is signed out.
Request logs
The same basic server records as the website (IP address, time, the action requested and errors). We use them to spot misuse, such as repeated failed sign-ins, and to fix problems.

What we don't collect

We don't ask for your name, address, phone number or payment details. The apps don't sell anything, so there's no checkout. We don't use advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use it

We use your information only to run the app for you: to sign you in, save and show your builds, check parts against each other, keep your account secure, and answer you when you contact us. Our legal basis is providing the service you asked for and keeping it safe.

Cookies

The website sets no cookies. A hobby app sets one cookie when you sign in, plus a matching security token that protects your account from forged requests. Both are essential: without them you can't stay signed in. They end when you sign out or the sign-in expires. We use no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, so there's nothing to opt out of.

Who we share data with

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share it only:

  • with service providers that host and run our site and apps for us ([hosting and email providers]), who may use it only to provide that service;
  • when the law requires it, such as a valid court order; and
  • to protect the safety of our users or the security of our services.

If TheHodgesPodges is ever sold or merged, user information may move to the new owner, who will be bound by this policy unless you agree otherwise.

Retailers and affiliate links

Our apps may show parts with a dated price from a retailer. We get those prices from the retailer. We don't send them anything about you.

Today, nothing on TheHodgesPodges is an affiliate link. In the future some retailer links may be, which means we may earn a commission if you buy after clicking, at no extra cost to you. If we add them, we'll update this policy and label those links. When you leave our site for a retailer, its own privacy policy and cookies apply.

How long we keep data

  • Your account and builds: for as long as your account is open. When you delete a build, its snapshots are deleted with it. When your account is deleted, we remove your account and builds within [30] days.
  • Sign-in records: until they expire or you sign out, then for up to [number] days for security review.
  • Server logs: up to [number] days.
  • Backups: copies of deleted data can remain in backups for up to [number] days, until those backups are replaced.

Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct it if it's wrong;
  • delete your account and everything in it.

You can also download your builds yourself from inside an app at any time. To make a request, email [contact email] from the address on your account. We'll answer within 30 days. We won't treat you differently for making a request. Depending on where you live, such as California or the European Union, local law may give you further rights, including to complain to a data protection authority.

Security

We protect your information with encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, private-by-default builds, limits on repeated sign-in attempts, and access to our systems restricted to the people who run them. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

Children

TheHodgesPodges is meant for adults and teenagers. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new date. If a change affects how we use information you've already given us, we'll tell signed-in users before it takes effect.
